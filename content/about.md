Accountable Impact Commons is a Lab at [LF Decentralized Trust](https://www.lfdecentralizedtrust.org/), part of the Linux Foundation. The Lab maintains the Anthropogenic Impact Accounting Ontology Suite, a semantic framework for representing anthropogenic impact accounting data in a machine-readable format.

The work is the product of collaboration by members of the [Standards Working Group of the LFDT Climate Action and Accounting SIG](https://lf-hyperledger.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CASIG/pages/825655298/Standards+WG+Home), and became a Lab in July 2026. The Lab is listed on the [LFDT Labs site](https://lf-decentralized-trust-labs.github.io/labs/approved/accountable-impact-commons.html).

We chose the name Accountable Impact Commons because we view these ontology resources as belonging to a global and open community.
