Accountable Impact Commons is a lab under [LF Decentralized Trust](https://www.lfdecentralizedtrust.org/), part of the Linux Foundation. The lab maintains open semantic standards for accounting for the impact of human activity on the environment, so that claims about impact can be stated, substantiated and audited in a machine-readable way.

The work originated in the Standards Working Group of the LFDT Climate Action and Accounting SIG.

## Maintainers

The lab is maintained by its contributors. See the [governance repository](https://github.com/Accountable-Impact-Commons/governance) for the current maintainer list.
