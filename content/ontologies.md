The lab maintains four complementary OWL 2 ontologies. Each has a persistent namespace, human-readable documentation and a source repository.

| Ontology | Namespace | Documentation | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impact Ontology | `https://w3id.org/impactont#` | [docs](/impactont/) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Accountable-Impact-Commons/impactont) |
| Anthropogenic Impact Accounting Ontology | `https://w3id.org/aiao#` | [docs](/aiao/) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Accountable-Impact-Commons/aiao) |
| Claim Ontology | `https://w3id.org/claimont#` | [docs](/claimont/) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Accountable-Impact-Commons/claimont) |
| Information Communication Ontology | `https://w3id.org/infocomm#` | [docs](/infocomm/) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Accountable-Impact-Commons/infocomm) |

**Impact Ontology** provides the foundational vocabulary for modelling impact: an activity, the environment it affects, and the change that connects them.

**AIAO** extends that foundation to human activity and the accounting of its effects, from greenhouse gas emissions to ecosystem outcomes.

**Claim Ontology** models defeasible claims, the agents that make them, and the substantiation that supports or refutes them.

**Information Communication Ontology** models the communication of information itself, so that reports, disclosures and messages can be described as events with senders, receivers and content.
