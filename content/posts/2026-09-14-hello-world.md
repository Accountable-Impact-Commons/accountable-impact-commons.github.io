Accountable Impact Commons is a lab under [LF Decentralized Trust](https://www.lfdecentralizedtrust.org/). It grew out of the Standards Working Group of the LFDT Climate Action and Accounting SIG, and it exists to answer a plain question: when someone claims their activity had an impact on the world, how can that claim be stated precisely enough to be checked?

Our answer is a small family of open ontologies that fit together:

- **[Impact Ontology (impactont)](https://w3id.org/impactont)** provides the foundational vocabulary for impact: the activity, the thing affected, and the change that links them.
- **[Anthropogenic Impact Accounting Ontology (AIAO)](https://w3id.org/aiao)** builds on that to describe how human activities are accounted for, from emissions to ecosystem effects.
- **[Claim Ontology (claimont)](https://w3id.org/claimont)** describes claims, who makes them, and the evidence that substantiates or defeats them.
- **[Information Communication Ontology (infocomm)](https://w3id.org/infocomm)** describes the act of communicating information, so that a report, a disclosure or a message can itself be a first-class object.

All four are published as OWL 2 ontologies under open licences, with documentation on this site and source on [GitHub](https://github.com/Accountable-Impact-Commons).

## What this site is for

This is where we will write about the work: design decisions in the ontologies, worked examples of impact claims expressed with them, tooling, and news from the lab. Posts are written by lab maintainers and contributors.

## Get involved

The lab is open. If you work on sustainability reporting, carbon accounting, impact measurement or the semantic web and want to help make impact claims accountable, open an issue or a pull request on any of our repositories, or join the LFDT community channels.
